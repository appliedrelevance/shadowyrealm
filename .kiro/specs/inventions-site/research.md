## Launch run, 2026-11-13

The full launch set (`content/inventions/index.ts`: 150 entities, 345 periods, 186 links) was loaded and every period scored. Totals run from 8.2 to 12,157.4.

The leaders are the oldest inventions still in use: the wheel, writing, the plough, irrigation, iron and bronze. That is the model working as specified: influence is level × years, and these have had five to eleven thousand years at a reach of most of humanity. ShadowyRealm shows the same shape, led by Ancient Egypt. Recent products with huge reach and little time, such as the iPhone and ChatGPT, rank low today and rise every year they last.

With the launch set loaded, every benchmark expectation still holds: Midgley's harm 106.4 against benefit 70.8; the transistor 149.3 against the Intel 4004's 22.7; the printing press 807.8 against the Walkman's 50.3; and Kilby, Noyce, Swan, Newcomen and Otto each draw more credit than their own.
